
- `background-color`: `any-other-color transparent currentcolor(current color of property "color") inherit(from parents)` 

- `background-image`: 
 - `url()`
 - `linear-gradient(direction, color1, color2, color3)` 
  - `using words` : `top to bottom, left to right, right to left, bottom to top`
  - `using angles`: `0(top) to 90(right) to 180(bottom) and so on` 
 -  `repeating-linear-gradient(deg, color1, color2, color3)`
 -  `radial-gradient(color1, color2, color3)`
 - `conic-gradient()`

- `background-repeat`: `repeat-x repeat-y no-repeat`

- `background-position`: `x% y%`

- `background-attachment`: `fixed scroll`

- `background`: Shorthand
