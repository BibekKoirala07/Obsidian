Just to be safe, whenever you are about to transfer data in AWS, **always assume a base rate of $0.09/GB unless proven otherwise.** So if you want to download 1TB of data from an EC2 machine over to your local machine, that will cost you $90 in data transfer costs alone.

There are many cases where you will not be charged, as we will see later in the course. But for now, please always assume that unless you **find proof in the documentation** to make sure you won’t end up with any extra costs.
