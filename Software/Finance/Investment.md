# What is an ETF?

This changed investing forever.

Suppose there are 500 of the biggest companies in America.

Instead of buying each one individually...

...you buy **one ETF**.

That ETF automatically owns all 500 companies.

Think of it like:

Instead of buying one fruit...

You buy the whole fruit basket.

---

Some famous ETFs include

- VOO
    
- VTI
    
- VT
    

Many long-term investors simply buy one of these every month for decades.

---

# Why do people love ETFs?

Because history shows something interesting.

Most professional fund managers fail to beat the market over long periods.

So many experienced investors simply buy the whole market.

No guessing.

No stock picking.

Just own everything.

## Here's one thing I think you're _not_ learning enough

Over our conversations, I've noticed you often ask:

> "What platform?"
> 
> "What investment?"
> 
> "Which broker?"

Those are useful questions, but the biggest difference in long-term results usually comes from:

- investing consistently,
    
- increasing your income,
    
- keeping costs low,
    
- staying invested for decades.
    

The platform matters much less than those habits.

And as a backend developer, you have one advantage many people don't: your career has the potential to increase your investing capacity dramatically over the next 10–15 years.

I think you should absolutely keep exploring global investing. Just remember that understanding **why** experienced investors choose broad, diversified funds is more valuable than simply getting access to an overseas brokerage. Once you have both the knowledge and a legal way to invest internationally, you'll be in a strong position to make informed decisions rather than chasing whatever is popular at the time.
