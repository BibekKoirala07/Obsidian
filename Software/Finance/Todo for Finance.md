## If I were building a finance library for you

### 1. _The Psychology of Money_ — The Psychology of Money ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Start here.

This book is not about stocks.

It's about:

- Why people become wealthy.
    
- Why smart people make terrible financial decisions.
    
- Risk.
    
- Compounding.
    
- Time.
    
- Behavior.
    

Finance is as much psychology as mathematics.

---

### 2. _The Simple Path to Wealth_ — The Simple Path to Wealth ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

This explains:

- Index funds
    
- ETFs
    
- Financial independence
    
- Why many investors don't try to beat the market
    

It's one of the clearest introductions to long-term investing.

---

### 3. _A Random Walk Down Wall Street_ — A Random Walk Down Wall Street ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

This is where investing starts becoming more rigorous.

You'll learn about:

- Efficient markets
    
- Asset bubbles
    
- Stocks
    
- Bonds
    
- Mutual funds
    
- ETFs
    
- Portfolio construction
    

Many university courses recommend it.

---

### 4. _The Intelligent Investor_ — The Intelligent Investor ⭐⭐⭐⭐☆

This is a classic.

It introduces ideas like:

- Margin of safety
    
- Value investing
    
- Investing versus speculation
    

It's influential, but it's denser than the books above, so I'd read it later.

---

### 5. _Common Sense on Mutual Funds_ — Common Sense on Mutual Funds ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Written by the founder of Vanguard.

It explains:

- Mutual funds
    
- Index funds
    
- Costs
    
- Long-term investing
    

This book changed how millions of people invest.

---

### 6. _The Little Book of Common Sense Investing_ — The Little Book of Common Sense Investing

A shorter, easier version of Bogle's philosophy.

Excellent if you're busy.

---

## Books about the financial system itself

Since you're asking questions like "What is Visa?" and "How does money move?", these are especially relevant.

### _Money: The True Story of a Made-Up Thing_ — Money: The True Story of a Made-Up Thing

One of the best books on:

- What money is
    
- Why money exists
    
- Banks
    
- Credit
    
- Bitcoin
    
- Modern finance
    

Very readable.

---

### _The Ascent of Money_ — The Ascent of Money

This is about the history of finance.

You'll learn how we went from:

- Gold
    
- Coins
    
- Banks
    
- Insurance
    
- Stock markets
    
- Global finance
    

It's broad and fascinating.

---

### _Broken Money_ — Broken Money

One of the best modern books on:

- Money
    
- Banking
    
- Payment systems
    
- Central banks
    
- Global monetary systems
    

It goes beyond investing and explains how the financial infrastructure works.

---

## Books that explain economics

These explain why economies function the way they do.
