Nouns is a word that names something like person, place, things, activity, quality or idea.
e.g.
- cat
- bicycle
- Taylor Swift

## Common Nouns
It is a nouns for not so particular person, place or any other things. like girl, river, insects. 
- It is not capitalized
- _The_ **_girl_** _crossed the_ **_river_**. Here "girl" and "river" are common nouns because we don't learn the identity of girl by reading though we know the action which she takes, which is crossing the river

#### Concrete Nouns
It is a noun which can be perceived by sense, it is physical. like ocean, river, doorbell, keyboard

#### Abstract Nouns
It is a noun that can't be perceived by sense. Like, courage, love

#### Collective Nouns
It is a noun that denotes a group of things. Like, pack 
- _That_ **_pack_** _of lies is disgraceful._ Here "pack" here is collective noun. Collective nouns take a singular verb, as they refer to the collection of people or things they identify as one entity
- The *pride* of lion is dangerous. Here "pride" is collective nouns and it is always singular as denote the whole entity

#### Countable Nouns
Countable nouns are nouns that can be counted, even if it is extremely high, like number of people in earth.
- can be singular or plural

#### Uncountable nouns
Uncountable nouns, or [mass nouns](https://www.grammarly.com/blog/mass-noun/), are nouns that are impossible to count, whether because they name intangible concepts (e.g., _information_, _animal husbandry_, _wealth_), collections of things that are considered as wholes (e.g., _jewelry_, _equipment_, the _working class_), or homogeneous physical substances (e.g., _milk_, _sand_, _air_).

## Proper Nouns
A proper noun is a specific name of a person, place, or thing. like Mount Everest, Kohli, etc.
- It is always capitalized

_Houston_, we have a *problem* . Here "Houston" is Proper noun while "problem" is common noun
